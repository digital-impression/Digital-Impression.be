How it started

It started with one cake.

Jessie came home from road trips through the States with one plan. "Naked Cakes started with the dream of doing Red Velvet right." Soft red layers, a proper frosting and nothing hiding under a thick coat of fondant. The rest of the menu grew around it.

House rules

Four little things that make Naked Cakes, Naked Cakes.

  1. No reservations

    Just walk in and grab a free table. First come, first served.

  2. The end-slice lottery

    Same price. No choosing. No begging.

  3. No custom cakes

    We bake what we love and do it well. Boundaries are sexy.

  4. Email beats DM

    Emails are safer if you want an actual reply. yummy@nakedcakes.be
